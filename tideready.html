Is TideReady for me?

If your day depends on the tide, yes.

TideReady answers one question: should I go to the coast right now? You get the answer, not a chart to interpret.

It's built for sea anglers deciding when to fish the tide, surfers checking if it's worth the drive, paddleboarders and open-water swimmers picking a safe window, kitesurfers reading wind against tide, and families and dog walkers who don't want to get caught out by an incoming tide. It works from official tide data (UKHO, NOAA, CHS, NIWA) across the UK, Ireland, US, Canada and New Zealand, and the safety warnings are free for everyone.
